About {{ APP_NAME }}

Designed with uncompromised local privacy, medical data sovereignty, and zero cloud lock-in.

🏥 Our Mission

The Medicine Reminder & Health Organizer was architected as a complete personal health command center. Modern healthcare involves managing complex schedules for multiple generations—from aging parents taking cardiac medications, to children requiring antibiotic dosages, to personal wellness and preventive care.

We believe your personal medical history, vitals, prescriptions, and symptom records belong exclusively to you and should never be commoditized by cloud surveillance platforms or exposed to data broker breaches.

🔒 100% Offline & Local Architecture

Unlike contemporary health apps that transmit sensitive medical data to external cloud APIs, this platform operates entirely on your local machine:

  • Zero External APIs: No third-party AI, notification gateways, or tracking scripts.
  • Local SQLite Database: All tables reside in an ACID-compliant local database.
  • Local Reminder Scheduler: Dose alarms and notifications compute in local Python runtime.
  • Deterministic Rule Engines: Safe, rule-based clinical health insights that never hallucinate.
  • Atomic Backup Subsystem: One-click local backup and disaster recovery archive creation.

⚠️ Medical Information Disclaimer

This software is an administrative health organizer and personal logging utility. It is not a medical device, does not offer medical diagnosis, and does not replace the professional judgment of qualified healthcare providers. Always consult your physician, pharmacist, or specialist regarding changes to medications, dosage regimens, or symptoms.
