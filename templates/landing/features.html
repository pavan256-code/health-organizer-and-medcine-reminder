Deep Specifications

Complete Architectural Modules

Explore each fully-featured domain included within the platform.

💊 Module 1: Medication Management

Full cataloging of prescription and OTC medications with generic name, strength, unit, formulation (tablet, capsule, syrup, inhaler), before/after food guidance, dosage frequency, inventory depletion velocity, minimum stock warnings, and batch expiry surveillance.

⏰ Module 2: Reminder & Adherence Engine

Deterministic local scheduler creating daily and weekly dose schedules. Record doses as Taken, Skipped, Delayed, or Snoozed. Calculates compliance percentages across medicines and family profiles with audio and in-app alerts.

🩺 Module 3: Clinical & Medical Records

Doctor specialist directory, clinic visits with reschedule/cancellation tracking, multi-item prescriptions, symptom journals with severity indices, vaccination history with upcoming due dates, allergy records, and protected local document storage.

❤️ Module 4: Vitals & Health Metrics

Record blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), fasting and postprandial glucose, resting heart rate, body weight, height, automatic BMI computation, body temperature, and oxygen saturation (SpO2) with interactive trend visualization.

🥗 Module 5: Lifestyle, Diet & Sleep

Log meals by type (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack), track water hydration in milliliters, record physical exercise duration and distance, and calculate nightly sleep duration and sleep quality.

💡 Module 6: Rule-Based Health Insights

A local, rule-based diagnostic guidance engine that correlates compliance drops, consecutive elevated blood pressure readings, low sleep averages, and critical drug-drug interaction warnings with safe informational context.

📄 Module 7: Reporting & Export Engine

Generates clinical multi-page PDF reports (via local ReportLab) complete with patient demographics, medication tables, adherence rates, and vital logs for doctor reviews, alongside raw CSV export files.

💾 Module 8: Audit & Disaster Recovery

Comprehensive request-level audit logging tracking every record creation, update, and deletion, paired with an atomic SQLite database backup and media ZIP archive generator.
